# Move tab

A tab belongs to the space it was opened in. When work starts in one checkout of a repo and continues in another (a worktree, say), Move tab sends the tab there with its Claude Code conversation resumed. Right-click a tab and pick `Move to…` (or press `cmd-shift-m` for the focused tab) and pick a checkout, or drag the tab onto a checkout in the Spaces tree, and a moment later the same conversation runs in that checkout's folder, in its space, with the old space left behind. Nothing to install besides this folder: no hooks, no shell startup edits.

## What you see

- **Menu item:** a `Move to…` row with the `git-branch` icon in the tab's right-click menu, just above `Close`, with `cmd-shift-m` on its right like the other items' keys. It is part of a copy of Herdr's `menu`, since the shipped one is read-only.
- **Popup:** opens next to the menu, 300 x 320 px, `popover` background, 5px padding, 2px between rows.
  - A `Move to` heading (the kit's `heading`).
  - One row per target, in a scrollable list: 8px side padding, 8px gap, radius `RADIUS.control`, minimum height `CONTROL.large`. Each row has a `git-branch` icon in `muted`, the label in `foreground` and, when it differs from the label, a second line at the kit's small size in `muted`.
  - The first row is on `selected`; a hovered row is on `hover`. The arrow keys move the selection (wrapping at both ends), Enter picks, Escape closes.
  - While it reads the checkouts it says `Looking for checkouts…`. With nothing to pick it says `No other checkout of this repo.`
- **Targets,** in this order:
  1. the main checkout: its space if it has one, else a row named after its folder;
  2. spaces open in a worktree, labelled with the space's name and detailed `<branch> · open`;
  3. worktrees with no space yet, by branch name, detailed with the branch.
  Every space open in the tab's repo on this machine is a row, extra spaces in the same checkout included, except the tab's own space. A space on another machine is never a row, even when its folder has the same path as a checkout here. A main checkout's open space is detailed `main checkout · open`; only its row with no space says plain `main checkout`.
- **Drop rows:** while a tab dragged from the tab bar is over a Spaces tree row that takes it, the row gets a 1px `accent` border (`.border_1().border_color(t.accent)`) that follows the row's own corners: radius 8 on graph rows in `vjr-desk`, none on block rows, and square everywhere in `vjr-desk-square`. While the drag is moving, every other row that takes it gets a dashed 1px `accent` border (`.border_1().border_dashed().border_color(t.accent)`), with the same corners; rows that don't take it look as usual.
- **After the move:** a toast titled `Moved to <branch or folder name>`, with the body `Claude's background jobs ended.` when a Claude was ended and no body otherwise; level success, 4 s. The target space and the moved tab are selected. In the old space the tab is gone, and when it was the space's only tab the space has a new tab with a shell in its checkout's root, so the space keeps its name and color.
- **When it can't:** a warning toast, `Can't move this tab`, with the reason (see Watch out), and nothing changes.
- The worker, a floating block, draws an empty `div()`.

## How it behaves

- **The key.** `cmd-shift-m` opens the popup for the focused tab in both layouts. `mod.open` gives the popup no `tab` in its props, so it takes `focused()`'s `tab` and `space`; a `tab` in its props, as the menu passes, wins. With no focused tab it draws nothing. The menu finds the key in `keys()` by its action, `mod.open:vjr-move-tab` (`mod.open:vjr-square-move-tab`), so it shows whatever key `keys.json` binds to that action.
- **Dragging.** Pressing a tab in the tab bar writes `move-tab.drag` to shared state: `{ tab, space, at }` (the tab, its space, `Date.now()`). Releasing it in the tab bar, on a tab or on the bar, clears it, after the bar's own select or reorder. Once the pointer is more than 4 px from where the tab was pressed (`Math.hypot` from the press's `e.position`, read by the bar's `on_mouse_move` while the tab is pressed), the bar adds `moving: true` to the drag, once; a plain click never does. While `moving`, the dragged tab draws at opacity 0.45, and the tab under the pointer (kept by each tab's `on_hover`) gets an `accent` border, since releasing there reorders the bar.
- **Which rows take a drag.** The drag is under 30 s old (`DRAG_TTL`), the row has a checkout, it isn't the drag's own space, and its repo (git common dir) is the one the drag's space is in. A repository row's checkout is its path and its repository the entry's; a row outside the tree of a repository (an extra space of a main checkout) uses its space's `git rev-parse --show-toplevel` and `--git-common-dir`. A space outside git, on another machine, or whose folder git hasn't answered for yet takes nothing.
- **Dropping.** The row lights up from `on_hover` and `on_mouse_move` while a drag it takes is in shared state, and goes back on hover-out. Releasing on it while it is lit (`on_mouse_up("left")`) writes `move-tab.request` with `to` = the row's checkout and `toSpace` = the row's space or `null`, and clears the drag. A release anywhere else in the list clears the drag too.
- **A press ends a drag.** Any press anywhere ends a drag older than 300 ms (`DRAG_GRACE`). In the Spaces tree, every row's `on_mouse_down("left")` and the tree's root clear `move-tab.drag` before anything else, and so do its icon buttons (the fold chevron, `+`, the header's buttons), which stop the press. Any press outside the tree's header (the `Spaces` bar above the list) reaches the header's `on_mouse_down_out`, which clears the drag only when it is older than 300 ms, so the drag the same press on a tab just started survives. That covers presses outside the tree and presses on the list's own scrollbar, which may stop the press before a row or the root sees it. So a click on a row after a tab was let go elsewhere opens the row (`space.select` or `space.new`), and a press that slides onto a row never moves the tab.
- **Asking.** The popup, or a drop on the Spaces tree, writes a request to shared state under `move-tab.request`: `{ id, tab, space, to, toSpace, at }` (`to` is the target checkout's path, `toSpace` the target space or `null`). It closes at once. The worker copy sees the request, clears it, drops it when it is older than 10 s, and runs the move. Only one move runs at a time; a second request gets the warning `Another move is running.`
- **What moves, per pane of the tab:**
  - **Claude Code:** its process is ended with `kill -TERM`, then it is resumed in the matching folder with `claude --resume <session id>`, the options it started with and, when the checkout changes, `--append-system-prompt` with a note: `This session moved from <old> (branch <name>) to <new> (branch <name>). Work in <new> from now on; files under <old> belong to another checkout.` (`detached HEAD` stands for a missing branch.) Claude is told its folder changed, and the conversation takes no extra turn.
  - **A shell with nothing running:** a new shell in the matching folder.
  - **A mod pane:** the same mod with the same props, in the new tab.
- **Matching folder.** The terminal's folder relative to its checkout's root, applied to the target's root (`main/src/app` becomes `worktree/src/app`). When that folder doesn't exist in the target (`test -d`), the target's root.
- **Which conversation.** The Claude's foreground pid names `~/.claude/sessions/<pid>.json`, which holds the live `sessionId` (it follows `/clear` and `/resume`). The options kept are `--dangerously-skip-permissions`, `--permission-mode <v>`, `--model <v>`, `--add-dir <dir…>`, `--verbose`, `--agent <v>`, `--effort <v>`, `--settings <v>`, `--mcp-config <v…>`, `--strict-mcp-config` and `--plugin-dir <v>`, also as `--opt=value`. Relative values of `--add-dir` and `--plugin-dir`, and of `--mcp-config` and `--settings` unless they are inline JSON (starting with `{`), are resolved against the old Claude's folder, so they name the same files from the new one; absolute and `~` paths stay as they are. Any other option is dropped, and so is one whose value, once resolved, holds a single quote, a backslash or a control character. A session id is used only when it is letters, digits and dashes.
- **Order.**
  1. Every pane is read before anything is touched: the tab's tree from Herdr's document, then each terminal with `hc get <t> --json`. A pane that can't move (working, blocked, busy, sub-agents, a sub-agent itself, no session) refuses here. The checkout checks come next: no source checkout, a target that is gone, or the tab already there.
  2. The checkouts come from `git worktree list --porcelain` in the tab's folder. The target and the source must both be among them.
  3. Each Claude is read once more (same pid, same session), signalled, and polled with `ps -p` every 250 ms until its process is gone, up to 10 s. The shell stays, since the Claude was its child. A Claude that is its terminal's own process (`pid` equals `foreground.pid`) has no shell behind it: when its tab is its space's only tab, `tab.new {space, folder}` gives the space a shell tab in its checkout's root before the signal, so the space outlives the tab that may close with the Claude.
  4. The target space is the requested one, else any open space of the target checkout, else a new one with `space.new {folder}` (its first tab is closed later).
  5. The new tab is made with `tab.new {space, folder, argv?, name?}` (or `mod`, `props` for a mod pane), named only when the old one had a name. The other panes follow the old tab's splits: `pane.focus` on the pane to split, then `terminal.open {place, folder, argv?}`, or `pane.split {direction, pane, mod, props}` for a mod pane.
  6. If the old tab is still its space's only tab and step 3 made no shell tab, `tab.new {space, folder}` gives the space a shell tab in its checkout's root. Then `tab.close {id, confirmed: true}` closes the old tab, and `space.select` and `tab.select` show the new one.
- **If something fails.** A refusal changes nothing, and neither does a failure before any Claude ended, besides the shell tab step 3 may have given the old space. `space.new` runs only after every Claude has ended, so a build that fails after it leaves the new space behind, holding Herdr's first shell tab (closed only once the move's new tab exists). After a Claude ended, a `Couldn't move the tab` error toast says why and gives `cd <folder> && claude --resume <id> <options>` for each Claude that wasn't started again. Its click action opens a new tab in the old space that resumes the first of them. A Claude whose own `tab.new` or `terminal.open` failed is never offered, since Herdr may have started it before the error: unless `terminals()` already shows a live terminal with `--resume <id>` in its argv or its foreground's argv (then it counts as started), the toast adds `Claude from <where> may already be running in <branch or folder name>.` After the new tab exists, a failure to close the old one gives a warning, `Moved the tab`, `<name> is now in <target>, but <reason>.`

## Built from

- `mod.json`: `{ "place": "none", "title": "Move tab", "overlay": { "kind": "popup", "width": 300, "height": 320 } }`. The overlay is how `mod.open` opens it from the key: a popup at the menu's size.
- **The recipe** (`recipes/move-tab.ts`), in both layouts:
  - copies `mods/move-tab/` and Herdr's `menu`;
  - adds to the menu's `tab` case `["Move to…", "move-tab", { tab: tg.id, space: tg.space }]` before `Close`;
  - makes choosing it run `openOverlay({ mod: "move-tab", kind: "popup", width: 300, height: 320, props: it[2] })`, and gives it the `git-branch` icon;
  - adds a floating block to the root, `{ "id": "move-tab", "content": { "mod": "move-tab", "props": { "role": "worker" } }, "edge": "left", "align": "end" }`;
  - sets `keys["cmd-shift-m"] = "move-tab"`. `apply-layout` writes it as `"cmd-shift-m": "mod.open:vjr-move-tab"` in `keys.json`'s `layout:vjr-desk` section and `"mod.open:vjr-square-move-tab"` in `layout:vjr-desk-square`, on top of the keys derived from `global`;
  - copies Herdr's `tabs` and patches it four times: its `herdr` import gains `setShared`; a tab's `.on_mouse_down("left", (e, cx) => { cx.stop_propagation(); this.pressed = i;` goes on with `setShared("move-tab.drag", { tab: c.id, space: space.id, at: Date.now() });`; the bar's `.on_mouse_up("left", () => { this.pressed = null; })` also runs `if (shared("move-tab.drag")) setShared("move-tab.drag", null);` and gains an `on_mouse_move` that sets `moving` past 4 px (the press also keeps `this.from = e.position`); each tab gains the faded and outlined looks and an `on_hover` that keeps `this.target`, drawn before its `on_mouse_down`; and a tab's release reads `const from = shared("move-tab.drag") ? this.pressed : null; this.pressed = null;`, so a tab dropped in the tree, whose release the bar never saw, can't reorder the bar later. A reorder inside the bar still works: the drag is set until the bar's own release clears it, after the tab's. The `git-graph-panel` and `command-center` recipes patch the same copy;
  - puts `const MOVE_TAB = { name: "move-tab" };` before the menu's `function menuIcon(command) {` (the generator prefixes the name), and makes the first line of its `function shortcut(name, args) {` read ``if (name === "move-tab") return keys().find((b) => b.action === `mod.open:${MOVE_TAB.name}`)?.keys ?? "";``.
- **Installed names:** `vjr-move-tab`, `vjr-menu` and `vjr-tabs` in `vjr-desk`; `vjr-square-move-tab`, `vjr-square-menu` and `vjr-square-tabs` in `vjr-desk-square`. The square copy is also listed in `recipes/square.ts` in `mods` and `OWN_MODS`, so its corners are squared and its popup is frameless with a 1px border, and in `FRAMELESS`, so its `mod.json` overlay gets `frame: "none"` and the popup the key opens is frameless too.
- **The Spaces tree** (`mods/spaces-tree/`, in both layouts as `vjr-spaces-tree` and `vjr-square-spaces-tree`) takes the drop. `tree.js` has `DRAG_TTL = 30000`, `DRAG_GRACE = 300`, `dropTarget(entry, row, look)`, which gives `{ path, common }` or `null`, and `accepts(drag, target, row, sourceCommon, time)`. `main.js` wires the main checkout's row, each worktree row and each row outside a repository's tree through one `pointer` method, which also carries the space rows' reorder; the narrow rail's rows and the root only clear the drag, the header forgets one older than `DRAG_GRACE` on any press outside it, and the tree's icon buttons clear it through the kit's `iconButton` `onPress`.
- `logic.js`: pure functions, no imports. `REASONS`, `base`, `parseWorktrees`, `checkoutOf`, `matchingFolder`, `parseSession`, `isShell`, `quote`, `keepArgs`, `resumeCommand`, `atShell`, `isClaude`, `movedNote`, `resumeArgv`, `runsSession`, `tabTree`, `leaves`, `splitSteps`, `placeOf`, `targetsFor`.
- `move.js`: `moveTab(request, io)`, the order above, with every effect behind `io` so it is tested with a fake.
- `io.js`: the real `io`. Programs: `git worktree list --porcelain`, `hc get <t> --json`, `sh -c 'printf "%s\n" "$1" | hc connect'` with `doc.get {name: "herdr-app"}` (the `sh` is only there to pipe into `hc connect`, which has no `doc` command), `test -d`, `ps -p`, `kill -TERM`, and `herdr-app do <command> '<json>'` for every change, so each command reports what it made or its error. It reads `~/.claude/sessions/<pid>.json` with `readTextFile`.
- `main.js`: the View. The kit: `heading`, `icon`, `CONTROL`, `RADIUS`, `TEXT`.

## Watch out

- **Refusals.** Nothing changes and a warning toast, `Can't move this tab`, says one of these (`where` is `this tab`, or `pane 2` in a split tab):
  - `Claude is working in <where>. Move it when the turn ends.`
  - `Claude is waiting for you in <where>.`
  - `<Program> is running in <where>.` for anything but Claude, an idle shell or a mod: a dev server, `nvim`, another agent, a shell with a program in front. The program is the name Herdr shows for the pane.
  - `Claude has sub-agents running in <where>.` (live terminals whose `parent` is that Claude).
  - `Claude in <where> is another agent's sub-agent.` (a sub-agent kept as a pane: its own terminal has a `parent`).
  - `Couldn't find Claude's conversation in <where>.` (no `~/.claude/sessions/<pid>.json`, or the id isn't safe to type, or the Claude changed since the check).
  - `Tabs on other machines can't move yet.`
  - `This tab isn't in a git repo.` (no checkout of the tab's folder was found). A repo with no other checkout just shows `No other checkout of this repo.` in the popup, with no request and no toast.
  - `That checkout is gone.`
  - `The tab is already there.`
  - `The tab is gone.`
  - `Another move is running.`
- **Claude's background processes end with it** (shells it started in the background, its MCP servers), as when Claude exits on its own. They can't be told from the MCP servers, so they don't block a move. The success toast says so in its body, `Claude's background jobs ended.`, and puts the place in its title, so both fit on the toast's one line.
- **The toast shows one line.** A failure notice puts the resume command in its body, which gets cut, so its click action resumes the conversation in a new tab of the old space. That tab is the way back; the command in the body is for copying.
- **A move cut off** by a layout switch or an app restart is not run again and gives no toast: the worker's task is cancelled and the session id goes with it. If Claude had ended, the old tab shows a shell where it was, and Claude's own `/resume` in the old folder lists the conversation.
- **Splits keep their shape, not their sizes.** The row and column tree is reproduced from Herdr's document; every split is made at Herdr's default size.
- **An original `--append-system-prompt` is not kept.** It is not in the options list, so a resumed Claude gets only the moved note (or none, when the checkout doesn't change).
- **A move between two spaces of one checkout** ends the Claude and resumes it in the same folder with no note, since nothing changed for it.
- **Claude may ask to trust a folder it hasn't seen.** The resumed Claude stops at that prompt in the new tab. Answer it there.
- **A Claude that is its terminal's own process** (started as the terminal's command, with no shell behind it, as `hc run … -- claude` adopts one) takes its pane with it when it ends, and its tab when that was the only pane. When that tab is its space's only tab, the space gets its shell tab before the signal, since Herdr closes a space with its last tab; if the Claude then doesn't exit, that shell tab stays. The new tab holds the resumed one, and the old tab is closed if it is still there.
- **The transcript stays under the first folder's project** in `~/.claude/projects/`. `claude --resume <id>` works from any folder, but the worktree's own `/resume` picker may not list the conversation.
- **Two Claudes never share a conversation.** The new one starts only after the old process is gone from `ps`. If it doesn't exit in 10 s, nothing moves, and the toast says `Claude in <where> didn't exit, so nothing moved.` A failure toast never offers to resume a Claude whose start was asked for, even when `herdr-app do` reported an error, since the terminal may exist anyway.
- Local spaces only: the popup lists no space on another machine, and the worker lands only in this machine's spaces.
- **A drag between blocks.** The tab bar and the Spaces tree are separate layout blocks. A press in one and a release in the other delivers the release to the row's `on_mouse_up("left")`, and the row gets `on_hover` and `on_mouse_move` while the button is held. The border is state the row sets from those events, not a `.hover(...)` style, which a drag from another block can't be relied on to show.
- **A tab let go anywhere else** (a terminal, a panel) leaves `move-tab.drag` set, since only the tab bar and the Spaces tree see releases, and their events carry no held button. Until the next press, for at most 30 s, the tab stays faded, the rows that would take it stay dashed, and they light up under the pointer.
- **`on_mouse_down_out` runs in the capture phase**, so for a press on a tab it may run before or after the tab sets the drag. The 300 ms grace is what keeps that new drag alive whichever comes first.
- **One registration per event.** A row registers `on_hover`, `on_mouse_move`, `on_mouse_down("left")` and `on_mouse_up("left")` once each, and a space row's reorder runs inside those same handlers: the kit doesn't promise that a second registration on one element adds to the first rather than replacing it.
- **The narrow rail** (a sidebar under 160px) takes no drops.
- Herdr's `run()` returns nothing inside a mod, which is why every change goes through `herdr-app do`.
- `pane.split` takes no folder, so a split terminal is made with `terminal.open {place, folder}` after focusing the pane to split.
- `apply-layout` rewrites both layout sections of `keys.json` on every run, so `cmd-shift-m` changed there by hand comes back. Change it in `recipes/move-tab.ts`.

## Done when

- `herdr-app check` passes on the mod folder, and `bun test` passes.
- Right-clicking a tab shows `Move to…` just above `Close`, with `cmd-shift-m` on its right, and picking it opens the popup with the tab's repo's other checkouts and no row for the tab's own space.
- Pressing `cmd-shift-m` opens the same popup for the focused tab.
- A Claude tab in the main checkout, moved to a worktree, shows up in that worktree's space with the same name. Asking Claude `pwd` answers the worktree, and the conversation continues.
- The main checkout's space no longer has the tab. When it was the only one, the space has a tab with a shell at the checkout's root.
- A tab with `python3 -m http.server 8765` running refuses with `Python is running in this tab.` (the name Herdr shows for the program), and nothing changes.
- A Claude that is working refuses with `Claude is working in this tab. Move it when the turn ends.`
- Dragging a Claude tab from the tab bar fades it, dashes every worktree row that takes it, and gives the row under the pointer a solid `accent` border; releasing there moves the tab as `Move to…` does.
- A click on a tab never fades it, and dragging a tab along the bar outlines the tab it would land on.
- The tab's own space and a space outside its repo never light up, and releasing on them moves nothing.
- After a tab is let go over a terminal, a click on a worktree row opens that worktree and moves nothing.
- The same holds in `vjr-desk-square`, with the popup square and frameless and the drop border square.
